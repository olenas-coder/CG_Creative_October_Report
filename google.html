1Summary

October 1 to 31, 2026 vs. September 2026

In October the account spent $1,983.87 and recorded 7 conversions: four Contact Form submissions and three Thank You page form fills. This is the strongest month of the last three, up from 6 in September and 0 in August, on the same level of spend.

Efficiency continued to improve. Cost per conversion came down from $335.53 to $283.41, conversion rate rose from 0.61% to 0.78%, and account CTR reached 6.26%, the highest in three months. Corporate Event Production produced five of the seven conversions at $129 each, and the Brand campaign added two conversions while lifting impression share to 85%.

The next step is directing more budget toward the searches that convert. "event production company" and "audio visual production company" both delivered results in October, and the Main Service campaign currently shows for fewer than 10% of eligible searches, so there is clear room to grow. Our priorities for the coming weeks are in Section 8.

What worked: Conversions rose to 7 on steady spend, cost per conversion dropped by 16%, and CTR climbed to its highest level in three months.
What we learned: Event production and audio-visual searches convert best; video production and Secondary Services bring solid traffic with room to convert more of it.
What we do next: Shift budget toward Corporate Event Production, expand the audio-visual keyword set and keep refining search terms (Section 8).

8Learnings & Next Steps

What October taught us and what we focus on next
✅ Worked

Seven conversions on steady spend

Conversions rose from 6 in September to 7, cost per conversion came down to $283 and CTR reached 6.26%, the best results of the last three months.

Next: put more budget behind what converts.
✅ Worked

Corporate Event Production and Brand

Corporate Event Production delivered five conversions at $129 each. Brand added two conversions at $107 each and grew impression share to 85%.

Next: grow reach for event production, keep brand coverage above 80%.
💡 Learned

Video and Secondary Services ready for a refresh

Corporate Video Production (253 clicks) and Secondary Services (214 clicks) bring strong traffic with conversions still to come. Many video clicks come from local "production company + city" searches.

Next: refine keywords, add negatives and refresh ad copy.
🎯 Priority 1

Shift budget toward Corporate Event Production (Recommendation)

Main Service shows for fewer than 10% of eligible searches. Moving part of the Secondary Services budget into Corporate Event Production gives the account's best converter more room to grow.

Proposed for November, first read in the next report.
🎯 Priority 2

Expand audio-visual and event planner keywords (Recommendation)

"audio visual production company" converted twice at $56 each and the search "corporate event planner" also converted. Adding close variants and testing a dedicated audio-visual ad group with tailored copy builds on this demand.

Keyword build-out and first read in the next report.
🎯 Priority 3

Search terms and negative keywords

We will analyze search terms every week and keep building negative keyword lists, with a focus on local video production searches, to keep spend on relevant corporate buyers.

Ongoing, every week.
Also on our radar: with 13 conversions across September and October, the account is building the conversion history needed to test a conversion-focused bid strategy on Main Service. Desktop converted at 1.13% in October vs. 0.70% on mobile, which we will monitor before proposing device bid adjustments.
