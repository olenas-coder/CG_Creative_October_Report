2Wins

What worked this month on each channel
Google Ads

Best month of the last three

7 conversions on steady spend (6 in Sep, 0 in Aug). Cost per conversion down 16% to $283; CTR 6.26%, a three-month high.

Google Ads

Event production is the engine

Corporate Event Production delivered 5 of 7 conversions at $129 each. "audio visual production company" converted twice at $56.

Google Ads

Brand coverage grew

Brand added 2 conversions at $107 each and lifted impression share to 85% (from 82%), so most CG searches now see our ad.

LinkedIn

Reaching leaders got cheaper

TOF image CPC fell to $24.19 from $27.11. Director + Event titles delivered clicks at $19.12, the lowest TOF cost in the account.

LinkedIn

Right roles, right industries

Directors and VPs rose from 66% to 78% of impressions. VPs click at twice the Director rate. Financial services engaged at the highest rates.

LinkedIn

Creative that lands

"Upgrade your events experience" hit 0.64% CTR; the personalized version beat standard by ~45%. Video view rate 41.9% vs. ~25% benchmark.

6Recommendations

What we recommend on each channel, from the October reports and the latest Google Ads data
Google Ads

Concentrate budget on the ad group that converts

Last 30 days: Main Service generated 4 conversions on $1,165.36 of spend. All 4 came from Corporate Event Production ($608.20 of spend).

Last 60 days: Corporate Event Production produced all 7 of the campaign's conversions.

1

Pause the top non-converting keywords

In the other Main Service ad groups, pause the keywords that spent the most without converting, and move that budget to the remaining keywords in those ad groups.

2

If those ad groups still do not convert

Allocate the entire Main Service budget to Corporate Event Production, the ad group that has consistently converted.

3

Tighten Secondary Services step by step

Presentation & Slide Design and Global & Enterprise Events Agency are the ad groups we could pause. First switch phrase match to exact match, then gradually pause non-converting keywords. Pause the whole ad group only as a final step.

Also from the October report: expand audio-visual and "corporate event planner" keywords, and keep adding negatives for local video searches.

Tie engagement to pipeline, then scale
