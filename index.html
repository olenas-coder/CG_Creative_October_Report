Impactable · CG Creative Studios · October 2026 Overview

Google event searches are producing conversions and LinkedIn now reaches the right buyers. Next we connect both to pipeline.

KEY RESULTS AND DRIVERS

  • Google: 7 conversions (6 in Sep) on flat spend, as budget moved to the converting keywords.
  • LinkedIn: CPC fell to $24.19 (from $27.11) after the VP and Director audience rebuild.
  • No LinkedIn conversion events (4 in Sep); retargeting reach fell 55–70%.

LEARNINGS

  • Event production and audio-visual searches convert. Video and Secondary Services searches bring clicks, not enquiries.
  • VP marketers, financial services and 1,001–5,000 employee companies engage most.
  • “Upgrade your events experience” is the lead ad message.

NEXT STEPS

  • Google: concentrate budget on Corporate Event Production; tighten or pause what doesn't convert.
  • LinkedIn: exclude low-engaging segments, test shorter video, run one test per funnel stage each month.
  • Use CRM data to keep audiences that match real opportunities.
What we need from you:  90-day CRM export  ·  full customer list  ·  short video cuts

2Focusing on event buyers and senior roles is working; lead visibility is the main gap

Wins and watch-outs across both channels

WINS

  • Google: conversions 0 → 6 → 7 on steady spend; cost per conversion down 16% to $283.
  • Google: Corporate Event Production won 5 conversions at $129 each; “audio visual production company” won 2 at $56.
  • Google: Brand impression share rose to 85%, with 2 conversions at $107.
  • LinkedIn: VP + Director share of impressions rose from 66% to 78%; they took 92% of cold-audience clicks.
  • LinkedIn: Director + Event titles delivered the lowest top-of-funnel CPC at $19.12.
  • LinkedIn: video view rate of 41.9% vs a ~25% benchmark; the “upgrade your events” ad led at 0.64% CTR.

WATCH-OUTS

  • LinkedIn: 0 conversion events (4 in Sep). The Conversions API is not yet receiving CRM events.
  • LinkedIn: retargeting reach fell 55–70% and frequency reached 11 to 25 on retargeting ad sets.
  • LinkedIn: viewers watch about 9s of a 64s video; 2.6% of views reach the end.
  • Google: Corporate Video Production spent $516 for 253 clicks and 0 conversions.
  • Google: Secondary Services spent $555 with 0 conversions, the same as September.
  • Google: Main Service shows for fewer than 10% of eligible searches.

6LinkedIn: narrow spend to the segments that engage and give the warm audience fresh creative

Plan for Nov 11 – Dec 11, by priority
HIGH

Use CRM data to choose audiences

Compare 90 days of contacts and deals with the companies, sizes and titles we reach; keep and grow the audiences that align.

HIGH

Exclude segments that don't engage

C-suite, Business Development, Operations and healthcare drew volume with little engagement. Add size and seniority filters to retargeting.

HIGH

New video angles, 15–30s cuts

The opening works (41.9% view rate) but only 2.6% of views finish. Test shorter edits against the current cut.

TEST

One creative test per funnel stage

Top: personalized vs standard ad. Middle: short vs current video. Bottom: “Learn more” vs “Request Demo”.

TEST

Weight toward 1,001–5,000 employees

This band clicked through more often than 501–1,000 in both cold audiences. Keep it if results hold.

TEST

Align targeting with CG's customer list

Map current and past clients to LinkedIn's industries to find strong-fit segments, and exclude existing clients from prospecting.
